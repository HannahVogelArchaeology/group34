    <!-- 1. Data We Collect -->

<section id="data-we-collect" class="policy-section">
  <h2 class="section-title">1. Data We Collect</h2>

  <div class="subsection">
    <h3 class="subsection-title">1.1 Information You Provide</h3>
    <p>We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when interacting with the Digital Exhibition, such as your name, email address and any information submitted through enquiry or contact forms.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="subsection">
    <h3 class="subsection-title">1.2 Information Collected Automatically</h3>

  </div>

    <!-- 2. How We Use Your Data -->

<section id="how-we-use-your-data" class="policy-section">
  <h2 class="section-title">2. How We Use Your Data</h2>

  <div class="subsection">
    <h3 class="subsection-title">2.1 Providing and Maintaining the Website</h3>
    <p>We may use collected information to operate, maintain and improve the Digital Exhibition, provide access to exhibition content, respond to enquiries and ensure that website features function correctly.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="subsection">
    <h3 class="subsection-title">2.2 Improving User Experience and Security</h3>
    <p>We may use technical and usage information to understand how visitors interact with the Digital Exhibition, improve accessibility and user experience, identify technical problems and help protect the website from misuse or security threats.</p>
  </div>
</section>

    <!-- 3. How We Share Information -->

<section id="how-we-share-information" class="policy-section">

  <div class="subsection">
    <h3 class="subsection-title">3.1 Service Providers</h3>
    <p>We may share limited information with trusted service providers that support the operation, hosting, maintenance or security of the Digital Exhibition. These providers should only use information as necessary to provide their services.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="subsection">
    <h3 class="subsection-title">3.2 Legal Requirements</h3>
    <p>We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety and security of the Digital Exhibition, its users or others. We do not sell visitors' personal information.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- 4. Your Choices and Obligations -->

  <div class="subsection">
    <h3 class="subsection-title">4.1 Your Privacy Choices</h3>
