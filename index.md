This is Group 34's public working site for an accessible archaeology exhibition
project. It is a student sandbox: its content and design will change as the group
develops and tests its response to the project brief.

## Start here

- Read the [project context]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}).
- Add each contributor's preferred public attribution on the
  [team page]({{ '/team/' | relative_url }}).
- Before adding media, record who owns it, the licence or permission that permits
  publication, and the wording of its credit.
- Treat accessibility as part of the design, not as a final correction.
