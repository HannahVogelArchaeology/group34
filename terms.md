## Credits are shown with each item

Every image, video, audio file, 3D model and piece of text is credited where it appears. For example, the caption under a photograph and the caption in the photo viewer give the credit line, such as "© Gale History Museum". The credit line names the rights holder. The rights in an item stay with the credited holder.
