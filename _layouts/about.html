Hannah Vogel is an archaeologist researching ableism and disability. She is an advocate for accessibility, with experience in museums, outreach and inclusive pedagogies. Hannah is a teaching academic and PhD candidate in the School of Humanities (Macquarie University) and was awarded emerging leader of disability by the National Disability Research Partnership.

Acknowledgements

This research was undertaken on the lands of the Wallumattagal clan of the Dharug Nation, whose cultures and customs have nurtured and continue to nurture this land, since the Dreaming. Their sovereignty was never ceded.
