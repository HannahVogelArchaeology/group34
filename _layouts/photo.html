Quick Guidance in the Photo Page

This is the section that explains how to use the photo page, before you view the photos.

If you already understand all of the instructions, press the Close guide button.

  • After closing this guide, press the Guide button on the top left to open it again.
  • Expand (top middle) shows the photo full screen. Press Esc, or the X button, to leave full screen.
  • Close guide (top right) closes this guide. After that, the Close button in the same place leaves the photo page and goes back to the previous page.
  • The caption of the photo is shown above the photo.
  • Click anywhere on the photo to see it larger. Click again, or press Esc, to go back.
  • The viewpoint of the photo and its position in the set are shown under the photo.
  • Press Previous, or the Left Arrow key, to view the previous photo.
  • Press Next, or the Right Arrow key, to view the next photo.
  • Click a small photo in the row at the bottom to see it. Move the row sideways with the mouse wheel or by swiping to see more photos.
