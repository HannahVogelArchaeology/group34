Quick Guidance in the Photo Page

This is the section that explains how to use the photo page, before you view the photos.

If you already understand all of the instructions, press the Close guide button.

  • To go back to the previous page, press the back button on the top left.
  • The total number of the photos is listed on the top middle.
  • To close the current page, press the close button on the top right.
  • The caption of the photo is listed above the photo.
  • Click the button on the left side of the photo to view the previous photo.
  • Click anywhere inside the frame to see more closely.
  • Click the button on the right side of the photo to view the next photo.
  • The number, object title, museum number, and viewpoint are listed under the photo.
  • Click any photo you want to see from the wardrobe. Scroll down to see more photos. Scroll left or right to see more photos.
