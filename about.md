Group 34 is working as a website team on an archaeology exhibition project. The
public repository records the group's content, design and decisions.

The project has no mandatory pull-request or approval process. Group members
will decide how to plan changes, review work, resolve conflicts and determine
when material is ready to publish.

Every commit to `main` is deployed automatically and becomes public.
