This repository is an isolated, public learning environment for Group 34. The
group is acting as a website team responding to an archaeology exhibition brief.
The repository and rendered site provide a durable record of that collaborative
work.

The client has deliberately not imposed a pull-request or approval process. The
group must decide how it will plan changes, review one another's work, resolve
conflicts, and decide when material is ready to publish.

The site deploys automatically whenever a commit reaches `main`. Every team
member should therefore understand that committed content becomes public.
