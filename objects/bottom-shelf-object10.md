transcript_text: "A likely wheel-made ceramic vessel. There is a handle arching over a flaring rim. The vessel has a narrow neck and wide, ovoid-shaped body tapering to a small circular base.  At the shoulder of the vessel is a spout, pointed upwards at an acute angle to allow the vessel to tilt. Several faded black painted lines can be seen as decoration around the handle, neck, and body. The spout was likely originally painted black in colour, although the surface is now eroded in places. The painted lines around the neck are tightly arranged, with a thick black line at the neck base. A thick, faded, wavy line extends around the shoulder of the vessel at the level of the spout."
